01 Developer tool

sw

Cross-machine verify and AI context sync

Verify your working tree on a second machine: uncommitted changes go straight to the peer to build and test, and your local AI session can come along too. No push needed, and the peer checkout stays untouched. LAN direct when possible, encrypted relay across networks.

Principles

How the software gets made

Local first

Whatever can be done on your machine stays there. The dictionary works offline, and game scores live only in your browser.

Small steps

Change one thing at a time and keep at it until it feels right. No rewrites for their own sake.

Quiet by default

No forced sign-up, no notifications, and no analytics built into the desktop apps. The games never flash or shake.
