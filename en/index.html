About

Three habits

Local first

If it can run on your computer, it stays there. The dictionary is fully offline, and game scores live only in your browser.

Small steps

Change one thing at a time and keep at it until it feels right. Each version is a little better than the last, never a rewrite for its own sake.

Quiet by default

No sign-up, no notifications, no nagging about updates. The games never flash or shake; when your eyes are tired, take a break.

Ideas, questions, a bug you found? Just write.

Every message gets read. Issues on GitHub work too.
