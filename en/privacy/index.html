Privacy

SDK Pods is a small independent studio. This page explains what data is involved when you use this website, the browser games, LingoDict and the sw command-line tool. Last updated 2026-11-23.

The website

This site is static. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and no contact forms. SDK Pods does not run its own analytics on the site, and we do not keep a database of visitors.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site (sdkpod.com) shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may show personalised ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites.

  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its advertising services, see policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • To opt out of personalised ads, visit adssettings.google.com.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google may show a consent message before ads load.

Browser games

The mini-games at /games/ store scores, settings and achievements only in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. Nothing from the games is sent to SDK Pods. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

The games also use two outside services:

  • Weather uses the free Open-Meteo API with an approximate location. By default this is a city chosen from your browser's time zone. Only if you click "use my location" does the game request your position, and then only coordinates rounded to two decimal places are sent.
  • Music is streamed from archive.org when you turn it on.

Those services receive the normal details of any web request, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Preferences

Your light or dark theme choice and your language choice are stored in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device.

LingoDict and the sw CLI

The desktop app LingoDict and the sw command-line tool contain no analytics and do not report usage to us. LingoDict only goes online when you choose to download the writing-check model, which is fetched from GitHub Releases. Everything else, including look-ups and vocabulary books, runs on your machine.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. As part of normal operation, their servers may log IP addresses and request details. Those logs are governed by GitHub's and Cloudflare's own privacy policies.

Changes to this page

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
